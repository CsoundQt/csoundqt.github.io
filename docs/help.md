## Troubleshooting

Have a look at the [Tips and Tricks](legacy/tips-and-tricks.md) page in the
[legacy documentation](legacy/index.md) if you experience an issue with
CsoundQt.

## Mailing list

You can get help with CsoundQt on the main Csound Mailing List, but for
CsoundQt specific issues, you can join the
[CsoundQt Users Mailing List](https://lists.sourceforge.net/lists/listinfo/qutecsound-users),
for ideas, bug reports and suggestions. This mailing list is also available
as a [Forum on Nabble](http://qutecsound-users.829572.n3.nabble.com/).

## Wiki

The [CsoundQt Wiki](https://github.com/CsoundQt/CsoundQt/wiki) offers a
number of helpful articles. It is also open for contributions by users -
create a new article and share your knowledge!

## Documentation

The up-to-date CsoundQt documentation lives at
<https://csoundqt.github.io/doc/>. The old (2015) manual pages are kept for
reference in the [legacy documentation](legacy/index.md).

## Bug reports and feature requests

Errors can be filed and wishes for enhancements described at the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/CsoundQt/CsoundQt/issues).
